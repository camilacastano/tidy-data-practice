# Tidy data practice (Case Study)

The goal of this project was to udnerstand a correct and useful way on how it is important to understand the data, its cleaning, manipulation and "tidying" before making and working on an analysis.

First, we did a complete lecture of the paper "Tidy Data" by Hadley to ahve complete context of the next steps of our analysis. Then, we study the dataset from Mexico 2008 related to all deaths throughout the year to detect outliers related to the variable "diseases"..
